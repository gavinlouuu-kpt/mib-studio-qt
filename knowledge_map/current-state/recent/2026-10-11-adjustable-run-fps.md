## 2026-10-11 — The Run frame rate is adjustable (bridge ABI 38)
Gavin: "The FPS should be adjustable." Run was fixed at 5 kHz / 150 us. Now `set_run_fps {fps, acknowledgeDiscardRing}` stores a whole number of frames per second from 100 to 5000 in the Aravis profile, `fetch_run_fps` reports the configured and applied rate, the derived exposure and the limits, and Studio's Run panel has a rate field (`RunRateControl`) that shows the exposure the rate implies.
- **Timing (frame rate only):** HMAX 58 is forced in Run (`options.pzHmax`, the producer would pick 116 below about 2.66 kfps) and the exposure stays the qualified 150 us (192 lines) at every rate; the producer computes VMAX = floor(74.25 MHz / (58 x fps)) and SHS = VMAX - 192: 5000 fps VMAX 256 / SHS 64 (unchanged), 1000 fps VMAX 1280 / SHS 1088. No PL, firmware or producer change, no LED change. Review ruling: adjustable FPS is not approval of a longer exposure or any illumination change, so the "derived exposure keeps SHS 64" mode was dropped.
- **Strobe vs exposure:** the exposure is the last 150 us of the frame, the strobe (7 us delay, 60 us width) is XVS-referenced: at 5000 fps exposure 50 to 200 us overlaps strobe 7 to 67 us; at 1 kHz exposure ~850 to 1000 us does not, so the frames would be unlit. `fetch_run_fps`, the readiness (`instrument.strobe`, Warn, does not block) and the rate field warn "strobe outside the exposure at this rate: frames will be unlit"; the overlap holds only above ~4.6 kfps.
- **Rules:** refused during an experiment or recording and while a stopped Run's ring is held unless acknowledged (the re-entry discards it); in Run the change re-enters Run at the same window; outside Run it applies at the next entry; the readiness gate fails if the camera runs at another rate than the configured one; the run snapshot records `run_timing`; the "5 kHz" texts follow the rate; the exposure shown is 150 us. The UI keeps "5,000 fps is the qualified rate; others are not yet validated on the board" until the 1 kHz slot passes.
