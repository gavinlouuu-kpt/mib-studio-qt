## 2026-11-04 — Compact mask exports

[[services/DotGridPortal]] now delivers compact combined DWG (when the provisioned
ODA converter is present) and dots-only OASIS, preserving the original device
and all pattern coordinates. DXF/GDS are secondary compatibility downloads.
Sizes are shown before download. All advertised artifacts must be stored before
readiness. Actual wafer round-trip and KLayout rendering evidence is recorded in
`docs/reports/2026-11-04-dot-grid-portal.md`; user-application compatibility is not
claimed without an application/version test.
