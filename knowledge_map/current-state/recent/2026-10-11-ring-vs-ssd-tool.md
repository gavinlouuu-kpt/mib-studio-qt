## 2026-10-11 — `tools/ring_vs_ssd`: the ring-vs-SSD comparison for M1 gate 5 (#693)
`tools/ring_vs_ssd/ring_vs_ssd.py` compares the ring frames Studio's playback served (saved `fetch_ring_frame` packets and `fetch_ring_status` with the ABI 37 `epoch`) with the same SSD run's export, by (epoch, frame_id): MONO8 bytes,
MASK1 bits, the 19 words of every RESULT cell, ticks and frame flags, over the run's final consecutive tail (criteria a-f in the README: tail equality, older frames equal or absent within guard + delta, a complete consecutive ring, the
overlap bound and the end distance, re-read equality after the export, flagged frames listed and excluded). Exit 1 on any FAIL, 2 on unusable input. It reuses the decoder vendored for `pzrec_to_h5`. 42 tests (CI: `tools/test_ring_vs_ssd.py`, CTest `tools.ring_vs_ssd`): synthetic packets and records, a mutant per
criterion, golden packets made by Studio's own `readFrame` + `buildRingPacket` from four real records (`tools/ring_vs_ssd/testdata`, kept equal to the reader by the C++ test `processing.ring_packet_golden`), and a smoke run on the export slot's real run 19. After the review of #697: a ring that overlaps nothing, or a run longer than a not-full ring, cannot pass; the re-read comparison looks only at the ring-defining status keys (`sensor_fps` is INFO). The slot (export bundle from develop, whole ring read after the run closes, export, re-read, 10 min idle) is described in the plan comments on #693.

- **Windows line endings:** `.gitattributes` now marks `third_party/pz7035-decoder/**` and `tools/ring_vs_ssd/testdata/**` `-text`. Without it a Windows checkout converted the vendored decoder JSON to CRLF and the
  PROVENANCE hash check failed (`tools.ring_vs_ssd`, and `pzrec_to_h5` on every Windows clone: a pre-existing bug). The full-ring check now keys on the frames the run offered (`seen`, or the frame-id span), not
  on those written, and `restore_needed` is a ring-defining key of the re-read.

**Content qualification:** the summary and `ring-vs-ssd.json` carry a CONTENT block over the compared frames (MONO8 min/max/mean/std and distinct values, constant-frame fraction, mask bits, cells) and classify images NONTRIVIAL/TRIVIAL (more than half of the frames above a 1.0 grey-level std floor), masks NONEMPTY/EMPTY and results NONEMPTY/EMPTY. A part that agrees only on trivial or empty content is called VACUOUS in its `a`/`b` lines and in `OVERALL PASS (VACUOUS: ...)` (exit code unchanged); `--require-content` fails it. So a dark-bench run cannot be read as evidence about masks or the cell path.

**Per-part acceptance:** `--require-content` takes `images,masks,results` (bare = all). `slot_driver.py` judges with `--require-content images` and writes `acceptance.json`/`acceptance.txt`: TRANSPORT PASS/FAIL, MASKS and RESULTS COVERED (content coverage, not validity) / VACUOUS / NOT ASSESSED, and "real-cell / optical acceptance: not assessed". Gavin 2026-10-11: a dark bench counts as transport and accounting only; the mask/results comparison stays open until a session with a sample flowing.
**Review of the acceptance:** TRANSPORT also needs pixels compared on every compared frame (no flagged cut / mask-incomplete / invalid frame), no constant frame and non-vacuous required parts; an unreadable report is a FAIL, `--no-judge` is NOT JUDGED, a part missing from the report is NOT ASSESSED; CONTENT counts frames identical to their predecessor (stale buffer). On the bench MASKS/RESULTS will likely read COVERED (the no-sample runs 18/19 had about 2 background objects per frame with mask bits): that is content coverage, not real cells.
