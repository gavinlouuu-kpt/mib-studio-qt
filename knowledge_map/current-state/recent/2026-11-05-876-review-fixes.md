## 2026-11-05 — Review of the Qt bench fixes: the Monitoring rings are byte-bound, OutOfRange is not a loss (#857 #859 #860)

The review of the PR for #857, #859 and #860 found #857 fixed only for a strict sub-ROI. Changes:

- **Monitoring retention is bounded by bytes.** Each ring keeps at most 32 MiB of row images
  (`ProcessingService::kMonitoringImageBytesPerRing`); past it the oldest rows give up their
  images and keep their measurements, the newest row always keeps its own. 32 MiB is 66 whole
  1216x208 rows per ring, against the 25 thumbnails and 24 strip cells the tab shows from the
  newest rows; the tab's rolling copies are trimmed to the same figure. A whole-frame or large ROI
  could pin 0.5 GB per ring under the count cap alone. The whole-frame crop is copied too: sharing
  the loop's buffers cost 267 page faults per frame against 16 (1.5x the frame time against 1.12x).
  [[services/ProcessingService]], [[frontend/ExperimentMonitoringTab]], [[diagnostics/MemoryBudget]].
- **`OutOfRange` is not a malformed frame.** A store without frames mid-pass (a `FrameStore`
  resize clears and renumbers it) ends the pass, as `NotYetCommitted` does, and the pointer resync
  handles it, in the inline and the async-batch loop; it was booked as `storeMalformed`.
- **Background calibration ignores frames the store could not give**, so a loop that fell behind
  does not use up its attempt budget on them.
- **A loss booked before the pass is no longer also a sequence gap** (`admitLostRange`).
- **Tests:** `processing.realtime_ring_loss` now covers the async-batch loop, drop-to-latest,
  `OutOfRange`, calibration and the byte budget; `performance.monitoring_row_latency` is the
  latency budget of the per-frame row; `frontend.camera_action_state` reads the Connect tab of a
  real `MainWindow` (#860).
- **Correction:** the earlier note said drop-to-latest already kept compact crops. That holds with
  an ROI; with no ROI it measures the whole frame, where the crop is the whole frame.
