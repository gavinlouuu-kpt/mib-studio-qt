| TD-18 | performance | On the rig PC (i9-13900, 32 logical CPUs), a headless 1000 fps soak burns about 16–18 cores at about 390 processed frames/s, against about 2 cores on a 4-vCPU Linux container at the same rate: about 41 ms vs 4.4 ms of CPU per processed frame.
